Each game pits 10 randomly-modeled agents (2 impostors, 8 crew) on The Skeld. Agents see only what their character can see — line-of-sight, recent events, their own task list. They reason in two steps (intent + theory-of-mind) before every action, and refresh their belief model after each meeting.
